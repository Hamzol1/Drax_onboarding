LinkedIn Poster — Privacy Policy

Drax Security · Effective date: 17 June 2026 · Application: LinkedIn Poster (Windows desktop)

This Privacy Policy describes how Drax Security (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the LinkedIn Poster desktop application (“App”). The App helps you compose, schedule, and publish content to LinkedIn using LinkedIn’s official APIs after you authorize access.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Drax Security
LinkedIn Company Page associated with the developer app: Drax Security
Privacy contact: privacy@drax.solutions
Security contact: security@drax-security.com

2. Summary

  • The App is local-first: your posts, drafts, media, and credentials are stored on your Windows PC.
  • We do not operate a Drax-hosted backend that receives your LinkedIn content or tokens.
  • The App communicates with LinkedIn (and only as needed with GitHub for optional updates and FFmpeg bootstrap) to perform functions you request.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Information we send to third parties

4.1 LinkedIn (required for core functionality)

When you connect an account or publish content, the App sends data to LinkedIn’s APIs, including:

  • OAuth authorization and token refresh requests
  • Post content, visibility settings, mentions, and media you choose to publish
  • Profile and organization lookups needed for authoring (e.g., people/org search when enabled)

LinkedIn’s processing is governed by LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy and LinkedIn API Terms of Use. You authorize the App through LinkedIn’s consent screen for scopes such as openid, profile, email, w_member_social, and optionally w_organization_social for organization posting.

4.2 GitHub (optional / technical)

  • Update checks (optional): The App may query the GitHub Releases API to notify you of newer versions. No post content or LinkedIn tokens are sent.
  • FFmpeg bootstrap (optional): On first video-related use, the App may download FFmpeg binaries from a public GitHub release to generate thumbnails locally. Media content is not uploaded to GitHub.

4.3 No Drax cloud collection

The App does not transmit your LinkedIn tokens, post bodies, or media to Drax Security servers. Drax Security does not use the App for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

5. Local storage and security

  • Application data directory: %LOCALAPPDATA%\LinkedInPoster\
  • Sensitive strings (OAuth tokens, client secret) are protected using Windows DPAPI tied to your Windows user profile.
  • OAuth redirect for authorization uses a loopback listener on http://127.0.0.1 on your machine only.
  • You are responsible for securing your Windows account, disk encryption, and backups of your PC.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above solely to:

  • Provide compose, preview, schedule, and publish features you request
  • Maintain drafts, restore interrupted work, and detect missed scheduled posts on your device
  • Refresh OAuth tokens and surface re-authentication when tokens expire
  • Enforce LinkedIn API quota awareness and show errors when limits are reached
  • Improve stability through local diagnostic logs

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data based on:

  • Performance of a contract / service: operating the App at your request
  • Legitimate interests: securing the App, preventing abuse of LinkedIn APIs, and local troubleshooting
  • Consent: your authorization via LinkedIn OAuth and your choice to install and use the App

8. Retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. You may delete individual posts/drafts, disconnect LinkedIn accounts, export backups, or wipe all local application data from Settings. OAuth tokens in backups are exported without secrets; restoring a backup requires re-authentication with LinkedIn.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data.

In the App:

  • Disconnect LinkedIn accounts in Settings
  • Delete posts and drafts in the Posts view
  • Export or import backups (Settings)
  • Wipe all local application data (Settings → data wipe)
  • Uninstall the App and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\LinkedInPoster\

Contact us at privacy@drax.solutions for privacy requests. We will respond within applicable legal timeframes.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International transfers

When you use LinkedIn, your data is processed by LinkedIn according to its policies and infrastructure. Local App data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up to a location you control.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in App release notes or this hosted document. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
